Spikeball (Roundnet)

Spikeball is 2-on-2 volleyball played 360° around a small trampoline-style net. I play on the BYU Spikeball team, and it is the most fun you can have with four people and twenty square feet of grass. Here's a crash course.

How a point works

  1. The server strikes the ball down onto the net toward the receiving team.
  2. The receiving team has up to three touches to return the ball to the net.
    • First touch: the dig — keep the ball alive
    • Second touch: the set — put it in your partner's hitting window
    • Third touch: the spike — bury it on the net
  3. Play continues until the ball hits the ground, the rim, or bounces twice on the net.
  4. Rally scoring — every point counts, games to 21, win by 2.
